Data interpretation

Net migration is the difference between immigrants and emigrants. A positive value indicates a net gain; a negative value indicates a net loss. It is not the gross number of arrivals and does not identify migrants originating from The Gambia.

Methodology and data limitations

  • Net migration = immigrants minus emigrants.
  • Positive values indicate net population gain through migration.
  • Negative values indicate net population loss through migration.
  • Historical estimates and future projections must be distinguished.
  • Future projections are scenario-based, not guaranteed outcomes.
  • National totals cover migration from all origins and to all destinations.
  • These figures do not measure irregular migration or arrivals from The Gambia alone.
  • Missing observations are displayed as unavailable, not as zero.
